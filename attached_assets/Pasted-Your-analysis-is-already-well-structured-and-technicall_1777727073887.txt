* Project-bound posts (Ships)

👉 This is production-grade thinking.

## 1. ❗ Cold Start Problem (Severe, Not Mentioned Strongly Enough)

Your system depends on:

* Projects
* Ships
* Engagement signals
* XP loops

But early users will face:

* Empty feeds
* No trending data
* No collaborators

👉 Without solving this, the system collapses regardless of engineering quality.

**Fix direction:**

## 2. ❗ Over-Engineered Personalization (Too Early)

You built:

* Behavioral tracking
* Interest decay models
* Hybrid ranking

But realistically:

* You don’t have enough data for meaningful personalization

👉 This is premature optimization.

**Better approach:**

---

## 3. ❗ Gamification Risk (Can Be Exploited)

XP systems are fragile.

Even with caps:

* Users can spam low-value Ships
* Collaboration requests can be gamed
* Comment farming is possible

👉 This leads to **Goodhart’s Law**:

> “When a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure.”

**Fix direction:**

* Introduce **quality-weighted scoring**
* Penalize low-engagement content
* Add reputation weighting

---

## 4. ❗ No Moderation = Platform Death Risk

You marked it “High,” but it’s actually **existential**.

Without moderation:

* Spam
* Abuse
* Low-quality flooding

👉 Your “Proof of Work” philosophy will collapse.

Minimum required:

* Admin dashboard
* Report system
* Content takedown tools
* Shadow banning

---

## 5. ❗ Dependency Risk: Multibase

This is a **single point of failure**.

If Multibase goes down:

* Image uploads fail
* Content becomes incomplete
* UX degrades instantly

👉 For a social product, media reliability is critical.
